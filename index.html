FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide health insurance guidance? +

Yes. SPS helps you understand family, individual and senior citizen health cover options, including what policies generally include, and how to choose based on your needs and eligibility. Policy benefits and claims are subject to the respective insurer's terms and approval.

Do you provide life insurance guidance? +

Yes. SPS provides guidance on protection and planning options such as term protection, family protection and related planning, explained clearly so you can make an informed decision. SPS does not guarantee policy benefits or claim outcomes.

How does nursing support work? +

SPS provides guidance and support to arrange appropriate nursing care, subject to availability and clinical requirements. Nursing support is coordinated based on your requirement, location and timing, and clinical suitability is decided by the treating doctor or a qualified professional.

What financial planning services are available? +

Guidance covers goal-based financial planning, including child education planning, retirement planning and wealth creation planning. This is educational, planning-focused guidance and does not guarantee returns or profits.

Can you help with retirement planning? +

Yes. SPS can help you think through retirement goals, future financial needs and suitable planning options based on your situation and time horizon.

Can you help plan for my child's education? +

Yes. SPS helps parents plan ahead for future education expenses and explore planning approaches based on their goals and priorities.

What does Health & Wellness guidance include? +

General awareness on nutrition, lifestyle, preventive wellness and everyday wellbeing. This is educational guidance only. SPS does not claim that any supplement cures or treats diseases, and does not replace advice from a qualified doctor.

Is the first consultation free? +

A free initial consultation may be available depending on the service. Please contact SPS through WhatsApp, phone or email to confirm current consultation availability.

What should I share before a consultation? +

Please share your name, contact number, location and a short description of your requirement. For insurance or financial planning, some additional information may be requested to understand your needs.

My question is not listed here — what can I do? +

Please reach out through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. If something requires confirmation, SPS will confirm the details with you rather than guess.
