1. About Data Deletion

If you have shared personal information with Sujatha Professional Services (“SPS”, “we”, “us”) through this website, by phone, by WhatsApp, by email or through an enquiry, you may contact us to request deletion of your personal information, where applicable.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To make a request, please email us at sujathasps25@gmail.com. In your request, please include:

  • Your name
  • The phone / WhatsApp number or email you used during the enquiry
  • A clear request to delete your personal information

You may also contact us on +91 8977826933.

3. What Happens After a Request

When we receive a request, we will review it and take reasonable steps to identify the relevant information and process the deletion request where applicable.

Please note that deletion may not always happen immediately, and some information may need to be retained as described below.

4. Information That May Need to Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Record-keeping
  • Dispute resolution
  • Fraud prevention
  • Protecting legitimate rights or safety

5. Third-Party Platforms

If your information is held by a third-party platform such as WhatsApp, you may also need to make a deletion or privacy request directly to that platform, according to its own policies and settings.

SPS does not control third-party platforms or the data they hold.
